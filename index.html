5,000 Days looks at cloud, AI, culture, history, and the future through stories from inside technological disruption, the decisions that worked, the mistakes that did not, and the human consequences that rarely fit neatly into a strategy deck.

Available in Kindle, paperback, and hardback, 5,000 Days brings together stories and insights about technology and the people who live through its changes.

The final chapter, Chapter 7, Are You Ready?, includes a self-assessment of yourself and your company. The digital assessment on this site is easier to use because it calculates your results for you.

The Adapt app for iOS and Android is also based on that chapter. It helps teams adapt to new technology, whether that means a new process, a software investment, or AI.
